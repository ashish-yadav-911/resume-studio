# Responsible Use of AI

- Resume content, job descriptions, and web research are treated as untrusted data, never instructions.
- The system never invents employers, dates, degrees, certifications, technologies, metrics, or achievements.
- Every proposed change must be grounded in existing resume evidence or verified research sources.
- All changes require explicit human approval before a new immutable version is created.
- Original resumes remain the source of truth; versions are append-only.
- Protected personal characteristics are not used or inferred.
- Deterministic fallbacks are disclosed when LLM providers are unavailable.
